
Demo account: `demo@halfbuilt.app` / `demo1234`

## Look and feel

- Type and neutrals follow rerun.io: system sans at weight 450 with tight tracking, JetBrains Mono for labels and buttons, hairline borders, small radii.
- Colour comes from soft baby pastels (peach, pink, lilac, sky, mint) and the city itself.
- The city is isometric 3D drawn in plain SVG (`src/iso/`): buildings, roads, lamps, trees, cars. No 3D library.
- Light and dark themes. Dark mode turns the city into a night scene.
